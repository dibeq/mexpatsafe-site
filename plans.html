Common Questions

Things expats always ask

Does Bupa cover pre-existing conditions?

It depends. Some conditions can be covered after a 2-year waiting period or via a premium surcharge — they are not automatically excluded. We walk you through the underwriting process during your consultation.

Does Medicare cover me in Mexico?

Medicare does not cover care outside the US. Bupa is specifically designed for expats living outside their home country — the coverage is built for your actual situation.

How fast can I get covered?

Once your application is submitted, Bupa typically processes in 5–10 business days. We manage the entire process from start to finish. General waiting period is 60 days, with exceptions for accidents and acute conditions from day one.

What is a high-deductible plan and who is it for?

A high-deductible plan means you pay a higher amount out-of-pocket if you ever need care, but your monthly premium is significantly lower. It is designed for healthy people who want catastrophic protection — not routine care coverage — at a fraction of the cost.
