What you should know about how RedChoice Care is regulated and about our role.

The insurer and how the policy is regulated

RedChoice Care is issued by Redbridge Insurance Company, Ltd., a company domiciled in Barbados and administered by Redbridge Group, LLC (Coral Gables, Florida). It holds a financial strength rating of B++ (Good) from AM Best.

This is an offshore major medical policy, regulated under the laws of Barbados. Any dispute relating to the policy is resolved under Barbadian law.

Our role

Diego Beltrán, operating as MexPatSafe (CNSF Y40634), acts as an intermediary of Redbridge Insurance products. We help you understand the options, prepare your application, and stay by your side.

Our role as intermediary does not create any legal responsibility for the fulfillment of the policy's conditions. All obligations relating to coverage, claims and the terms of the policy rest entirely with Redbridge Insurance Company, Ltd.

A few practical points

  • Premiums are quoted and paid in US dollars.
  • As an offshore policy, premiums are not deductible for Mexican income tax (ISR).
  • Coverage is worldwide, with lifetime renewal guaranteed and free choice of provider outside the United States.
  • Pre-existing conditions must be declared and approved at application to be covered.
  • Certain services require notification and pre-certification within 72 hours — we'll guide you through it.

This page is a plain-language summary. The Redbridge policy conditions and Schedule of Benefits are the governing documents and prevail in case of any difference.
