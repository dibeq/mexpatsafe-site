Meet your advisor

Diego Beltrán

I've been working in the Mexican insurance industry since 2022, and what started as a career became a calling. Today I serve a portfolio of over 100 active clients — most of them expats from the United States, Canada, and Europe who chose Mexico as their home.

This work requires deep technical, contractual, and legal knowledge. Navigating corporate and hospital bureaucracies on behalf of a client — especially when they don't speak the language — takes experience and patience. I've built both. My goal is simple: that every person I work with feels they have a friend in their corner, not just an advisor.

My connection to the expat world is personal. My wife Andy holds dual Mexican-German nationality, and I have family spread across different parts of the world. I genuinely love working with people from different cultures — it's what makes this career meaningful to me.

Outside of work, you'll find me on the golf course, traveling with my wife Andy, or chasing around our two beautiful girls. Life is good — and it's even better when you don't have to worry about what happens if it isn't.

So let's meet. Book a call, shoot me a WhatsApp, or — who knows — we might end up organizing a traditional carne asada together. Either way, I'm here for it.
